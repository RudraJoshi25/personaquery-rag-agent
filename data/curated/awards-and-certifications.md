### UOWx Record — Indigenous Nationals Event Assistant
- **Institution:** University of Wollongong
- **Role:** Event Assistant, Indigenous Nationals
- **Hours:** 20

Indigenous Nationals is a week-long multi-sport competition for Indigenous
student athletes. Event Assistants support Event Managers with daily
operational tasks and competition management: venue set-up and pack-down,
equipment tracking, merchandise sales, accreditation and scoring. The role
develops cultural awareness, teamwork and problem-solving skills.
